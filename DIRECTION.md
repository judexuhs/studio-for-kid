# Pebli — colorful creative toys

THESIS: Children arrange tangible, colorful pieces to direct their creative crew.
OWN-WORLD: User selected LEGO × Nintendo: bright toy colors, full rounded forms, tactile buttons. This replaces the rejected quiet Apple style. Seed 155fb1d2 yields to that pinned direction; no branded characters or marks copied.
STORY: Yellow ideas feed green Moss jobs; creations live in coral picture frames; blue Pip jobs bring them to life.
FIRST VIEWPORT: The canvas is the entry. First-time users see a nonmodal inspiration panel amid visible tools and crew; returning users resume their recent canvas. New idea opens that same panel. Projects live in a side drawer. Starting places linked idea and task cards in the workspace.
FORM: Color encodes object/partner roles; generous radii and short soft depth make cards feel graspable. Buttons visibly depress. Existing free canvas mechanics stay unchanged.
FINISH: Gabarito rounded lettering, dark readable ink, bright restrained palette, consistent controls and reduced motion. Mobile retains card navigation and readable task scale.

Code-led implementation; existing generated illustrations unchanged. No new capability or generation claims.
