Working decision rules (project heuristics, not validated industry thresholds): repeated pronunciation divergence, preschool-only associations, or mistaken identification with an existing product are reasons to pause a candidate. For a five-family pilot, aim for at least four independent first-read pronunciations close to intended and at least three delayed recalls. These small counts detect friction; they do not predict market success. Legal/domain objections cannot be averaged away by a high preference score. If none clears, keep the brief and generate a new round rather than select a weak winner.
